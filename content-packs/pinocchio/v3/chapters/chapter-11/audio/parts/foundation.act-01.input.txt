[British accent]
[hushed]
Pinocchio left the Tunny and followed the small light through heavy darkness.
Cold, greasy water covered the floor under his wooden feet.
The Shark’s breathing sounded like faraway thunder.
The golden light seemed like a star inside a black sky.
Pinocchio kept walking because waiting in one place could not help him.
Soon he saw a candle burning inside a glass bottle.
It stood in a bottle on a little table.
Boxes and wet barrels from a broken ship lay all around.
An old man sat beside the candle and ate a small fish.
His white hair and thin face looked familiar.
Pinocchio moved closer through the dirty water and held his breath.
The old man slowly looked up at him.
Pinocchio knew that the man was Geppetto.
[with astonished joy]
His wooden knees shook with sudden joy, and he almost fell.
“Father, I have found you!” he cried.
Geppetto rubbed his eyes in great surprise.
Pinocchio threw his arms around Geppetto’s neck.
They laughed and cried together in the weak light.
For a moment, neither one could speak clearly.
Geppetto touched the puppet’s face as if checking a wonderful dream.
Pinocchio promised not to leave him again.
“Are you really my Pinocchio?” Geppetto asked.
“Yes, and I searched everywhere for you.”
The Shark moved, and the candle shook above the small table.
Still, their dark corner now felt like home.
Then Geppetto finally asked about Pinocchio’s long and difficult journey.
[honestly, gathering momentum]
Pinocchio began with the book bought for school.
He admitted selling it for the puppet show.
He told Geppetto about Fire-Eater and five coins.
Then he described the clever Fox and Cat.
He spoke about the false Field of Wonders.
He also confessed his lies and growing nose.
The Blue Fairy had saved him many times.
However, Pinocchio had broken many promises.
He told Geppetto about prison and the watchdog job.
He spoke about school fights and the sea.
On Busy Bee Island, he had started working.
Then Lamp-Wick had invited him to Toyland.
Pinocchio had chosen play and become a donkey.
“I chose fun before love,” he said sadly.
[wearily]
Geppetto held his son close and forgave him after the story.
Then he explained why he was inside the Shark.
He had sailed out to search for Pinocchio.
A strong storm broke his little boat.
The Terrible Shark swallowed him with the pieces.
At first, Geppetto thought he would die there.
Later, the Shark swallowed a ship with food.
The ship also carried candles and matches.
Those things kept Geppetto alive for two years.
He had counted time by each burning candle.
Now only a little food was left.
The candle on the table was also the last.
