[tenderly]
Master Cherry wrapped it and gave it to Geppetto.
On the way home, Geppetto imagined a happy crowd.
He saw the puppet dancing while people clapped.
His real home was only one cold room.
A fire painted on the wall gave no heat.
Geppetto put the block on his workbench.
Then he lit a small lamp.
“What name shall I give you?” he wondered.
After a moment, he smiled.
“Your name will be Pinocchio.”
The name sounded bright and full of fun.
Geppetto made his tools sharp and ready.
“Let us make a boy and a family,” he said.
[playfully]
First, he carved the hair, head, and two eyes.
The new eyes looked straight at him.
“Please look kindly at your father,” Geppetto said.
The eyes turned up towards the ceiling.
Next, Geppetto made a long nose.
He cut it shorter, but it grew again.
Then he carefully shaped a mouth.
The mouth smiled and started laughing at him.
“You have no body, but you are already rude.”
The smile became even wider.
Geppetto carved the neck, body, arms, and hands.
The new hands quickly took his yellow wig.
Pinocchio waved the wig and laughed loudly.
“Give it back,” Geppetto said with a sad voice.
He finished the wooden legs and feet.
[with rising excitement]
Then Geppetto placed Pinocchio on the floor.
Pinocchio kicked his nose and pulled away.
One step became two, and then he started running.
He knocked over a stool and jumped across the bed.
“Stop!” Geppetto cried.
Pinocchio opened the door and ran outside.
He raced past shops, chickens, and laughing children.
Geppetto followed in slippers and called his name.
Pinocchio did not turn around.
Being free felt exciting, and danger felt far away.
[with quiet concern]
A police officer stood in the middle of the road.
He caught Pinocchio by his very long nose.
The puppet’s wooden legs kept running in the air.
Geppetto arrived tired but happy to see him.
“Thank you for stopping my boy,” he said.
“I will take him safely home now.”
Geppetto reached for ears that he had never carved.
He held Pinocchio’s neck, so the puppet fell down.
People thought Geppetto was hurting the wooden boy.
The officer listened to the crowd, not the facts.
He let Pinocchio go and took Geppetto away.
Pinocchio ran free while his father went to prison.
“I made you with love, but not enough sense.”
Pinocchio’s first hard lesson was now very close.
