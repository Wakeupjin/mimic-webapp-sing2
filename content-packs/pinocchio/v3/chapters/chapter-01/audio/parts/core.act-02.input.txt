[British accent]
[tenderly]
Master Cherry quickly wrapped the block and placed it in Geppetto’s arms.
As Geppetto walked home through the narrow streets, he imagined the puppet bowing before a delighted crowd.
He imagined music, applause, and warm meals shared beside the fire.
His real home was only one small room beneath a staircase.
A painted fire on the wall had to pretend to keep him warm.
Geppetto set the wood on his workbench and lit a little lamp.
“What shall I call you?” he wondered.
After a moment, he smiled.
“I shall call you Pinocchio.”
The name sounded bright, quick, and full of adventure.
Geppetto sharpened his tools with great care.
“Let us make a boy,” he said, “and perhaps a family too.”
[playfully]
Under the lamplight, Geppetto carved the hair, forehead, and two lively eyes.
The moment the eyes appeared, they stared directly at him.
“It is polite to look kindly at your father,” Geppetto said.
The eyes rolled towards the ceiling.
Next, he carved a nose.
The nose grew longer and longer, no matter how often Geppetto shortened it.
Geppetto sighed and continued.
He shaped a mouth, and the mouth immediately began to grin.
Then it laughed at him.
“You have not even got a body, and already you are rude.”
The grin only widened.
Geppetto carved the chin, neck, shoulders, arms, and hands.
As soon as the hands were finished, they snatched away his yellow wig.
Pinocchio waved it like a flag and laughed.
[with rising excitement]
“Give that back,” Geppetto ordered, though sadness had begun to replace his anger.
He finished the puppet’s body, legs, and feet, wiping away a tear as he worked.
When Geppetto placed him on the floor and took his hands, Pinocchio kicked his father’s nose and wriggled free.
One step became two, and two steps became a wild dash around the room.
Pinocchio kicked over a stool, leapt across the bed, and raced towards the door.
“Stop!” cried Geppetto.
The puppet pulled the latch and shot into the street.
He ran between market stalls, startled chickens, and laughing children.
Geppetto hurried after him in his slippers, calling his name.
Pinocchio did not look back.
Freedom felt too exciting, and consequences were still a mystery.
[with quiet concern]
A police constable heard the commotion and stood in the middle of the road.
Pinocchio tried to slip past, but the constable caught him by his remarkable nose.
His wooden legs continued running in the air.
Geppetto arrived, breathless and relieved.
“Thank goodness,” he said.
“He is my boy, and I shall take him home.”
Geppetto reached for Pinocchio’s ears, then discovered that he had forgotten to carve them.
When he caught the puppet by the neck instead, Pinocchio threw himself onto the road and refused to move.
The crowd misunderstood the scene and began accusing Geppetto of treating his son cruelly.
The constable listened to the loudest voices instead of examining the facts.
He released Pinocchio and took Geppetto by the arm.
Pinocchio raced away while Geppetto was led to prison as evening settled over the town.
“Oh, Pinocchio,” he sighed, “I made you with love, but I forgot to give you sense.”
And somewhere ahead, the wooden boy’s first hard lesson was already waiting.
