# Pinocchio v1 image-generation record

## Generation mode

All images were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool. The
saved production copies are in `assets/`; generated originals remain in the
tool output directory.

## Canonical prompt

> Use case: illustration-story. Create a production-ready 16:9 Living
> Storybook key stage in an original, non-franchise cut-paper-and-gouache
> language: visible paper fibers, hand-painted edges, muted forest green,
> terracotta, amber, deep teal, and warm theatrical light. Use one coherent
> wide tableau, exactly one instance of each recurring character, broad
> uncluttered camera-focus regions, and negative space for learning UI. Match
> the attached character reference. No text, letters, captions, logo,
> watermark, photorealism, montage panels, split frames, or recognizable film
> adaptation styling.

## Character reference prompt

The reference sheet established an original maple-wood Pinocchio with carved
dark hair, cream shirt, forest-green vest, rust shorts, visible joints, and
bare wooden feet; a thin white-haired Geppetto in ochre and charcoal; a sturdy
red-nosed Mastro Cherry in burgundy; a natural olive-brown Cricket; a huge
charcoal-bearded Fire Eater in plum; a lean russet Fox in moss; and a
charcoal-gray Cat in indigo.

## Chapter scene prompts

1. Mastro Cherry, one plain log, and the tools in his old workshop.
2. Geppetto and Mastro Cherry handing over the strange log.
3. Geppetto's room opening to the village chase and waiting officer.
4. Pinocchio listening to the Cricket, with the hammer resting safely nearby.
5. Pinocchio holding the two halves of one egg as one chick flies away; no
   other eggs, shells, baskets, or food props.
6. Pinocchio asleep near the stove while the storm continues outside.
7. Geppetto offering one pear to Pinocchio; exactly two more pears on the
   table and no other fruit.
8. Geppetto giving Pinocchio a plain new primer inside their small room.
9. Pinocchio at the school-or-music crossroads with generic human musicians,
   a ragpicker, and four coins.
10. Pinocchio reunited with the wooden theatre company on one paper stage.
11. Fire Eater embracing Pinocchio while another wooden performer waits in
    relief and the troupe watches.
12. Pinocchio meeting the limping Fox and peeking Cat at a forked road, five
    coins in hand, with a Blackbird, golden tree, and distant home.

The chapter-specific prompts inherit the canonical constraints and the full
character descriptions in `style-guide.json`.
