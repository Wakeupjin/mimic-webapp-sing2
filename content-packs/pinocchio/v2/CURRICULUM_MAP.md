# Twelve-session curriculum map

1. **A Wooden Boy Wakes** — original Chapters 1–3. A talking log becomes
   Pinocchio; irresponsible freedom sends Geppetto to prison.
2. **Hunger, Home, and Sacrifice** — Chapters 4–8. Pinocchio rejects advice,
   suffers hunger and lost feet, then recognizes Geppetto's sacrifice.
3. **The Puppet Theater** — Chapters 9–11. He sells the primer, enters the
   theater, and makes his first courageous choice for a friend.
4. **Five Coins and a Dangerous Road** — Chapters 12–15. Fox and Cat exploit
   his hopes and lead him past repeated warnings into the night attack.
5. **The Blue Fairy and the Field of Wonders** — Chapters 16–19. Rescue,
   medicine, lies, the growing nose, repeated fraud, robbery, and prison.
6. **The Watchdog and the Search for Father** — Chapters 20–23. A failed theft
   becomes an honest watchdog choice; news of Geppetto sends him into the sea.
7. **The Island of Busy Bees** — Chapters 24–26. Work, reunion with the Fairy,
   school progress, and a believable temptation connected to his father.
8. **Trouble at School and a Second Chance** — Chapters 27–29. A fight and
   false arrest lead to reciprocal rescue, patience, and rebuilt trust.
9. **The Land of Toys** — Chapters 30–32. He abandons his almost-human future
   for endless play and becomes a donkey.
10. **The Donkey and the Sea** — Chapters 33–34. Circus exploitation, injury,
    underwater transformation, and the Terrible Shark.
11. **Inside the Terrible Shark** — Chapter 35. Pinocchio finds Geppetto,
    confesses the journey, and carries his father out through the open mouth.
12. **A Real Boy at Last** — Chapter 36. Daily work, study, care, and sacrifice
    complete the moral transformation before the body changes.

Original Chapters 1–36 are covered exactly once, in order.
